Model Pulse and Decoherence

What is it?

The unimaginatively named Model Pulse and Decoherence project makes movies of nuclear wavepackets responding to time-dependent electromagnetic fields. It solves the time-dependent Schrödinger equation in a Gaussian discrete variable representation, a localized basis that makes the quantum dynamics visually interpretable. The top-right panel shows the laser field, and the bottom-right panel shows the potential energy surfaces colored by the position-space density on each electronic state. The left panels show the corresponding ground- and excited-state Wigner quasiprobability densities, which represent the wavepacket in phase space and connect naturally to classical motion. The side-by-side finite-pulse and continuous-wave examples highlight how changing the field duration changes the excited-state wavepacket motion.
