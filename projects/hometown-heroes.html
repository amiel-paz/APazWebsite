Hometown Heroes

Website

What is it?

Hometown Heroes is an interactive geospatial web app for visualizing where MLB, NFL, NBA, and NHL athletes were born, went to school, played college sports, and competed professionally. Use it to stoke your hometown pride, or to satisfy your curiosity about which regions produced which greats.

The app includes 82K+ athletes and 286K+ player-location events across the Big 4 U.S. sports leagues, with locations mapped around the world. It supports radius search, timeline and accolade filters, player cards, and source attribution throughout.
